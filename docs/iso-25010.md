# ISO/IEC 25010 software quality evaluation

System: AGILE-OUS Membership and Student Welfare System. Evaluated on November 23, 2026, against the product quality model of ISO/IEC 25010:2023.

The evaluation has two parts:

1. **Technical evaluation**: automated tests and measurements on the running system (sections 1 to 4). Anyone can repeat them.
2. **User evaluation**: a questionnaire for members, officers, MSW staff and IT evaluators (section 5).

## 5. User evaluation questionnaire

For respondents after they use the system for their role: members and applicants, officers (Committee Heads, Executive Board), the MSW team, and IT evaluators (faculty or IT professionals). IT evaluators answer every part; other respondents answer parts A, D, E and F.

**Scale:** 5 Strongly agree · 4 Agree · 3 Neutral · 2 Disagree · 1 Strongly disagree

**A. Functional suitability**
1. The system has the functions I need for my role (applying, tracking, asking for help, deciding, recording).
2. The system gives correct results (status, decisions, member records, IDs).
3. The functions help me finish my tasks without extra steps.

**B. Performance efficiency**
1. Pages open quickly.
2. Uploading documents and pictures takes reasonable time.
3. The system stays fast when many records are listed.

**C. Compatibility**
1. The system works on my browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. The system works on my phone as well as on a computer.
3. Downloads (reports, IDs, certificates, calendar files) open in common apps.

**D. Interaction capability (usability)**
1. The pages are easy to understand at first use.
2. I can find what I need from the menu.
3. Error messages tell me clearly what to fix.
4. The design is pleasant and consistent across pages.
5. The text is readable and the controls are easy to use.

**E. Reliability**
1. The system works without errors during my use.
2. My entries are not lost when something goes wrong.
3. The system is available when I need it.

**F. Security**
1. I trust that my personal data and concerns are kept confidential.
2. Only the right people can see or change my records.
3. Sign-in and email codes make me feel my account is protected.

**G. Maintainability** (IT evaluators)
1. The code is organized and easy to follow.
2. A change in one part does not break other parts (automated tests).
3. Rules that may change (by-laws, retention, IDs) can be adjusted in settings or configuration.

**H. Flexibility / portability** (IT evaluators)
1. The system can be installed on a typical PHP and MySQL host by following the guide.
2. Settings for a new environment are in one place (`.env`).
3. The system can move to another server without code changes.

**I. Safety** (IT evaluators)
1. The system warns users before actions that cannot be undone.
2. The system points users in danger to emergency help.
3. Demo or test data cannot mix with real records in production.

### Computing and interpreting the results

For each item and each characteristic, compute the weighted mean:

  weighted mean = Σ(f × w) / N

where *f* is the number of respondents who chose a rating, *w* is the rating (1 to 5) and *N* is the number of respondents.

| Weighted mean | Verbal interpretation |
|---|---|
| 4.21 to 5.00 | Strongly agree (Excellent) |
| 3.41 to 4.20 | Agree (Very good) |
| 2.61 to 3.40 | Neutral (Good) |
| 1.81 to 2.60 | Disagree (Fair) |
| 1.00 to 1.80 | Strongly disagree (Poor) |

Report the weighted mean per item, per characteristic, and overall, separately for each respondent group.
